# Problem Statement

Deep Learning has achieved significant success in areas such as image classification, natural language processing, speech recognition, and generative AI. However, traditional deep learning architectures often face challenges such as high computational requirements, difficulty in learning complex features, vanishing gradients, limited generalization, and dependency on large amounts of labelled data.

The selected research article addresses a specific problem in advanced Deep Learning or sequence modelling by proposing an improved architecture or learning approach. The main problem is to determine how effectively the proposed model can learn meaningful patterns and representations from the given data while achieving better performance than existing techniques.

The study focuses on analysing the limitations of conventional approaches and evaluating whether the proposed Deep Learning architecture can improve important performance measures such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, loss, computational efficiency, or generalization capability.

Therefore, the problem addressed in this article review is:

> **How can an advanced Deep Learning architecture or sequence modelling technique be designed and trained to overcome the limitations of existing approaches and achieve improved performance on the target task while maintaining effective learning and generalization?**

The article will be critically reviewed based on its problem definition, objectives, methodology, model architecture, dataset, training process, experimental results, comparison with existing techniques, strengths, limitations, and possible future improvements.
